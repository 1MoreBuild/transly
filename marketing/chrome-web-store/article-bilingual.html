Why context changes translation quality

为什么上下文决定翻译质量

Good translation is not a sequence of isolated sentences. Meaning develops across an article: a term introduced in the opening may shape every paragraph that follows.

好的翻译并不是一连串彼此孤立的句子。文章的含义会逐步展开： 开篇引入的术语，可能影响后续每一个段落。

Transly reads broader article context, preserves links and structure, and places each translation beside the passage it belongs to.

Transly 会结合更完整的文章上下文，保留 链接与结构，并把译文放回对应的原文旁边。
